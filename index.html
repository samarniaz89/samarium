SAMARIUM

Privacy policy · Last updated November 2, 2026

Samarium is a private assistant app used only by its owner, Muhammad Samar Niaz, to run his own businesses (Chaiiwala of London, Erin Mills and Heavenly Desserts, Mississauga) and his personal affairs. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What Samarium accesses

With the owner's permission, Samarium connects to the owner's own Google accounts (Gmail, and Google Business Profile for his restaurant) to read and organise email, reply to email he approves, and manage his business listing and customer reviews.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide these features to the owner.
  • It is stored on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens never leave it except to talk to Google.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Samarium's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect any account inside Samarium, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
